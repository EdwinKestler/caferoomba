## Publication discipline

Do not say “fine-tuned Cosmos,” “trained on the real dataset in Colab
Enterprise,” “deployed the learned policy on Jetson,” or “autonomous sweeping”
until actual records support those statements. The current live teacher refuses
execution; the human-only training baseline does not consume teacher annotations.

At `cd0bb2d`, full-data learning is `implemented` / `tested_offline` with
generated and synthetic inputs, and Stage 1 software integration is closed at
that same evidence level. The owner's synchronized/calibrated demonstrations,
reviewed labels, actual current-notebook Colab execution, live Cosmos, Orin
ARM64/TensorRT, physical commands, navigation and docking remain open.

Keep historical prototype dates, software revisions, learning completion, site publication and later tests distinct. Never backdate code or later execution evidence to an entry deadline. Contest rules and eligibility are determined by the organizers, not by this repository.
